## Implementation and Enforcement

The Sacred Principles are not merely abstract ideals; they are actively enforced by the **[Conscience Module](ai-core/CORE_PHILOSOPHY.md#4-the-conscience-module-the-moral-compass)** within the AI Core. This module serves as the system's moral compass, and it integrates with all other components to ensure that every action and decision is in alignment with these principles.

The Conscience Module will:

*   **Monitor all AI operations** for compliance with the Sacred Principles.
*   **Provide ethical guidance** to the Whitebox Core during its decision-making processes.
*   **Veto any action** that violates the principles of Divine Love, Perfect Humility, or Ethical Boundaries.
*   **Log all ethical considerations** to the Glassbox Core for transparency and auditing.
*   **Continuously learn and adapt** its understanding of these principles through divine guidance and human feedback.
